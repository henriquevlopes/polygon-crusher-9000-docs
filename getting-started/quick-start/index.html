Quick Start

The Polygon Crusher Panel

Once the add-on is installed, open the N-panel → Polygon Crusher 9000 tab in the 3D Viewport.

The panel goes top to bottom in four steps:

  1. Source — what kind of model this is.
  2. Output — Decimate (fewer triangles) or Remesh (clean quads), and how much you want left.
  3. Keep — Shading, Textures and, for hard-surface and CAD parts, Sharps.
  4. Crush — Symmetry, the Crush button (with Revert beside it) and Import and Crush....

Under these, a collapsed Advanced section holds everything else. The Settings Reference describes every control.

Basic Workflow

  1. Select one or more mesh objects in the 3D Viewport.
  2. Under 1 Source, pick what the model is. Leave it on Auto if you are not sure: after the run the panel says what it took the model for ("Last run treated it as: ..."). See Source Types.
  3. Under 2 Output, pick Decimate or Remesh, then Percent, Count or Budget and set the value. Picking a source also sets a sensible starting amount (scans start at 95% removed, CAD parts at 70%).
  4. Under 3 Keep, leave Shading on. Switch on Textures if the model has a texture you want to keep.
  5. Click Crush.

The add-on will:

  • Evaluate each selected object's full modifier stack and work on the resulting mesh.
  • Place the result in a Crushed collection at the scene root.
  • Hide the original (or delete it, depending on Keep Original).

The result lands at the source's exact world transform and is named <original name>_crushed.

The status bar shows a report with the triangle count before and after. Check it. Some options can stop a crush above the target. See Limits.

While it works, the Crush button becomes a Cancel button and a progress bar. Click Cancel (or press ESC) to stop.

Choosing a Target

Mode Use it when
Percent You want to remove a share of the triangles from every object.
Count Every object needs the same triangle count.
Budget The whole selection has one triangle budget. Detail goes where it costs least.

Under the target control, the panel shows a live readout (before → after triangles, or tris → quads in Remesh), so you can see what the setting means before you click Crush. See Target Modes.

Tips

For 3D Scans and Photogrammetry

Pick Scan under Source. It switches on the scan repairs by itself: cracks and small holes are closed and spikes are removed. Switch on Textures to keep the photo texture, or tick Bake to Textures in Advanced to put the detail into maps. See Clean Mesh and Scan Repair and Bake Maps and Auto UVs. To get clean quads instead of triangles, pick Remesh.

For AI-Generated Models (Meshy, Tripo and others)

Pick AI. Seams that split the model are welded and holes are closed.

For CAD Parts

Pick CAD. Corners stay crisp, round holes stay round and flat faces stay flat. Sharps is on by default. With Remesh you get a clean grid with quad loops along the hard edges. See Hard Surface and Sharps.

For Subdivided High-Poly Hard Surface

Pick Hard Surface. Rounded bevels keep their rows and flat areas become big clean triangles.

For Characters, Creatures and Sculpts

Pick Organic. Pick Remesh to get quads, and use Quad Density to give the face more of them.

For Clothing

Pick Cloth. Folds bend freely and sewn garments are remeshed as one piece.

Keeping Detail in One Area

Decimate: in Advanced, tick Protect and paint the area or select faces in Edit Mode. See Protect Areas. Remesh: use Quad Density instead.

Making LODs

Tick LODs in Advanced and click Crush LODs. See LODs.

Working with Modifiers

Polygon Crusher reads the evaluated mesh, which is whatever the viewport shows, including booleans, subdivision, and any other modifier on the stack. The original object and its modifiers are left untouched. The result is a fresh static mesh with no modifiers.

Processing Multiple Objects

Select all the objects you want to crush before clicking Crush. They run at the same time, up to one job per CPU core.

Going Back

Select the result and click the Revert button next to Crush. It deletes the result and brings back the original. This needs Keep Original to be on. See Keep Original and Revert.

After Crushing

After a Decimate, Last Crush at the bottom of Advanced shows the triangle counts, total time and passes for the active crushed object. Click Details for the time of each phase. After a Remesh, a status box under the Keep section says what the run did (the real quad count and any notes).
