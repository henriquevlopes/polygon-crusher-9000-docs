Hard Surface and Sharps

Organic meshes (scans, sculpts) can be crushed freely. Hard-surface and CAD parts cannot. Holes must stay round, panel lines must stay straight, and material borders must stay where they are.

What is kept automatically

With a source of Auto, CAD or Hard Surface, these are kept in place by default, and the result still reaches the count you asked for:

  • Edges sharper than the Hard Edge Angle (default 30°) and edges you marked Sharp.
  • Borders between materials (not in Shape only mode, see below).
  • Open borders (holes and cut edges).
  • UV seams, when Textures is on.

A kept edge is a cost, not a lock. The crush spends its budget everywhere else first, and when it has to touch a kept line it collapses along the crease rather than across it, so the line stays a line.

Organic, Scan and Cloth sources do not keep angle-sharp edges, because on those models they are noise or folds. Edges you marked Sharp are still kept.

Use Show Hard Edges (Advanced) to see the kept edges before you crush. See Show Hard Edges.

Sharps

Sharps is a toggle in the Keep row. It shows for Hard Surface and CAD sources, and is on by default (since 2.12.16).

With Sharps on, hard edges stay in place and stay crisp after Decimate or Remesh, including corners modelled with a small bevel (a game weapon's triangle bumps, long grooves and panel lines no longer go soft).

  • Decimate holds those edges harder while reducing, then marks the result's real creases Sharp and shades each side flat.
  • Remesh gets one quad loop per hard edge. Small round holes come out round instead of pinched into stars, and nothing folds.
  • Rounded parts and fillets that should look smooth are left smooth.
  • When bevel edges cover most of a low-poly model (the game weapon), they ease off, so the result still follows the shape.
  • It is skipped on sources over 1 million triangles (too slow there).

Turn Sharps off if you want the plain method.

Shape only

If you switch Shading and Textures both off, you get the shape only: a closed mesh ready to 3D print. The panel says "Shape only: closed, ready to print". Clean Mesh and Fill Holes (with a big hole size) switch on by themselves, and material borders are not held. They go back as they were when you switch Shading or Textures on again.

What a very low target costs

Reaching the count is not free. Every kept edge is a line the crush would rather not touch, and the more of them there are, the sooner a low target forces it to. On a low-poly part with many creases, a target of a few hundred triangles means some of those lines give way: a hole loses a few sides, a chamfer flattens. The crush picks the lines that change the shape least, but check the result.

If a line you care about gave way:

  • Raise the target.
  • Raise Hard Edge Angle, so fewer edges count as sharp.
  • Put a Protect Area on the part that must stay.

See Limits for the full list.

Decimate of CAD and subdivided models

  • CAD source: crisp corners and long thin triangles are handled, so round holes stay round and flat faces stay flat, with no streaks coming out of hole rims.
  • Hard Surface source (a subdivided high poly): crushed with a method that keeps the rows of rounded bevels and puts big clean triangles on flat areas. A 5-million-triangle gun body takes half the time it used to. See Source Types.
  • Flat Areas as Quads and Tidy Flat Areas (Advanced → Edges) clean up flat areas of a Decimate result. Flat Areas as Quads merges the triangles of each flat area into one face and pairs the rest into quads. Tidy Flat Areas flips long thin triangles into even ones without moving a point. They show for Auto, CAD and Hard Surface, in Decimate only.
