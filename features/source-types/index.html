Source Types

Step 1 of the panel asks what kind of model you have. The right method for that kind is used, and each kind starts at a sensible amount.

There used to be a Mode menu (Auto, Scan, Sculpt, Hard Surface, Custom). It was removed in 2.9.3: it only ticked checkboxes, sometimes behind your back. Source replaced it in 2.11.24 and works differently: it picks the method, and what it switches on is shown to you.

The seven sources

Source Use it for Starting amount (Percent)
Auto Not sure. The model is looked at and the method picked for you. unchanged
Scan 3D scans and photogrammetry: noise, cracks, holes. 95% removed
AI AI-generated models (Meshy, Tripo and others): split seams, baked texture atlas. 90% removed
CAD CAD parts (STEP exports): crisp corners, long thin triangles, exact holes. 70% removed
Hard Surface Subdivided high-poly hard surface: rounded bevels instead of crisp corners. 75% removed
Organic Characters, creatures and sculpts: smooth, no hard edges. 90% removed
Cloth Garments made of sewn panels. 85% removed

You can change the amount afterwards. The starting amount is set only when you pick the source.

CAD and Hard Surface are separate on purpose. CAD means crisp corners and exact round holes. Hard Surface means a subdivided high poly with rounded bevels.

Auto

Auto looks at each model and picks the method by itself. After a run, the panel shows a line under the buttons: Last run treated it as: ..., so you can see what it decided. If it guessed wrong, pick the source yourself.

What each source does

  • Scan and AI switch on Clean Mesh and Fill Holes by themselves (and Remove Spikes for Scan). They switch them off again when you change the source. Scans and AI models are also faster than Auto, because the model no longer has to be analysed first. See Clean Mesh and Scan Repair.
  • CAD and Hard Surface show the Sharps toggle under Keep and keep hard edges in place. See Hard Surface and Sharps. A Hard Surface source is crushed with the method made for subdivided hard surface: rounded bevels keep their rows, flat areas become big clean triangles, and the points stay exactly on the original surface. It steps aside by itself, and the usual method runs, when it does not fit: with Textures on, several materials, Symmetry, protected areas, keeping under 5%, or low-poly models it cannot bring down to the target.
  • Organic, Scan and Cloth do not keep angle-sharp edges: on those models a sharp-looking edge is noise or a fold. (Edges you marked Sharp yourself still count.) The Hard Edge Angle setting is hidden for them.
  • Cloth lets folds bend freely, so soft garments keep their shape.
  • CAD and Hard Surface with Remesh try a clean grid first. See Remesh (Quads).

Some settings only show for the sources they apply to. Tidy Flat Areas and Flat Areas as Quads show for Auto, CAD and Hard Surface. Cut Floor and Remove Spikes show for Auto, Scan and AI.
