Hard Surface Preservation

Organic meshes (scans, sculpts) can be crushed freely. Hard-surface and CAD parts cannot. Holes must stay round, panel lines must stay straight, and material borders must stay where they are.

The Preserve options keep those lines in place while everything else gets crushed. They are all off by default.

The options

Option What it keeps in place
Borders Open borders: holes and cut edges. They do not shrink or wobble.
Materials The borders between materials.
UV Seams UV seam lines, so textures do not smear across them. Needs Keep UVs.
Sharp Edges Edges you marked Sharp, plus any edge sharper than Sharp Angle.

The crushed mesh also gets those sharp edges marked Sharp.

One-click setup

Hard Surface in the Mode menu turns on all four options and sets Sharp Angle to 30°. Auto picks it by itself on a mesh with flat faces and sharp edges. See Modes.

You can also switch options on one at a time. For example, turn on only Materials to keep material borders on an organic mesh.

How kept edges behave (2.7.1)

Up to 2.7.0 a kept edge was a hard lock: the crush was not allowed to touch it at all. That had two costs. On a model with many borders, seams and creases (an electricity pole made of 103 parts) the crush ran out of edges it was allowed to collapse and stopped at about half the triangles, no matter what you asked for. And on a CAD block, the locked rims of the holes pushed every collapse into the faces next to them, so round holes came out as pentagons.

Since 2.7.1 a kept edge is a cost instead of a lock. The crush spends its budget everywhere else first, and when it has to touch a kept line it collapses along the crease rather than across it, so the line stays a line. In practice:

  • Hard Surface always reaches the count you asked for. The pole crushes to 2% with its structure intact; on the CAD block at keep 10% the holes stay round and the surface error is half of what it was.
  • Small parts no longer vanish at heavy reductions. Every connected piece keeps a minimum number of triangles until the error budget is truly spent. Before, at keep 2% whole beams of the pole disappeared while small insulators floated on; now pieces go in order of how little they matter, smallest first, and only once nothing else is left to remove.
  • Models with UVs no longer stop short of the target. With Low Distortion on (Hard Surface and Scan modes use it), the crush used to give up at around 12% above the target because it refused every collapse that touched a seam or might fold a UV face. It now tries the plain collapses first and only relaxes the UV rules, in stages, once the model is truly out of safe ones.

Scans, sculpts and smooth CAD parts crush the same as before (within measurement noise). See the Benchmark for the numbers.

Sharp Edges and Sharp Angle

Sharp Edges uses two sources:

  1. Edges you marked Sharp in Blender.
  2. Edges where the two faces meet at more than Sharp Angle (default 30°).

Bumps from scan noise do not count as sharp. An edge over the angle only counts when it sits next to flat faces or bends one way, like a real crease.

On a mesh that reads as a scan (mostly noise, few flat faces), the angle test is skipped. Only the edges you marked Sharp are kept. The report names the objects where this happened. See Limits.

UV Seams

UV Seams needs Keep UVs to be on. If Keep UVs is off, the UV Seams button is greyed out.

What a very low target costs

Reaching the count is not free. Every kept edge is a line the crush would rather not touch, and the more of them there are, the sooner a low target forces it to. On a low-poly part with many creases, a target of a few hundred triangles means some of those lines give way: a hole loses a few sides, a chamfer flattens. The crush picks the lines that change the shape least, but check the result.

If a line you care about gave way:

  • Raise the target.
  • Turn off the Preserve options you do not need, so the budget goes to the lines that matter.
  • Raise Sharp Angle, so fewer edges count as sharp.
  • Put a Protect Area on the part that must stay.

See Limits for the full list.

Presets

Presets remember the four Preserve options and Sharp Angle. Save one preset for hard-surface work and one for organic meshes.
