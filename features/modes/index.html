Modes

Which switches suit a model is not obvious: a scan wants Clean Mesh and the scan repairs, a CAD part wants its edges kept in place, a sculpt wants neither. The Mode menu at the top of the panel picks them for you.

Auto

Auto is the default. When a mesh is selected, Auto asks the engine for a one-second look at it and shows what it sees under the menu, in plain words:

Looks like a scan: rough surface all over Found: 120 spikes, 11 small holes

It then applies the matching mode (Scan, Sculpt or Hard Surface) and, for a scan, switches on only the repairs it actually found: Cut Floor when there is a table or ground layer under the model, Remove Spikes when there are spikes or stretched faces, Fill Holes when there are small holes. Clean Mesh comes on when the mesh is split along its seams or full of collapsed faces, in any mode.

How the engine decides:

  • Hard surface: many flat faces meeting at sharp edges, or the long thin triangles of a CAD export.
  • Scan: a rough surface everywhere (the faces around a typical point tilt by 8° or more) and almost no flat patches.
  • Sculpt: everything else, which is to say a smooth surface.

Auto looks again whenever the selected mesh's triangle count changes, and only in Object Mode. With several meshes selected it looks at the active one and applies the result to the whole crush.

Meshes over 600,000 triangles do not get looked at by themselves (it would take a few seconds on every selection). A Detect button appears instead; a crush in Auto mode always looks first anyway.

Auto's guess can be wrong on a mesh between two kinds (a smoothed hard-surface model, a very clean scan). Pick the mode yourself in that case.

Scan

For 3D scans and photogrammetry. Clean Mesh on, with Remove Spikes and Fill Holes (and Cut Floor when a table was found), Keep Normals, Keep UVs with Low Distortion when the mesh has UVs. Nothing is kept in place: scan noise has no edges worth keeping. The Clean Mesh and Preserve groups open in Advanced.

Sculpt

For sculpts and smooth organic models. Keep Normals and Keep UVs (when there are UVs), nothing else forced. The Protect Area and Bake groups open, because a face to protect and a normal map to keep the detail are what a sculpt usually needs.

Hard Surface

For CAD and mechanical models. Borders, Materials, UV Seams (when there are UVs) and Sharp Edges at 30° are kept in place, with Keep Normals and Keep UVs (when there are UVs). Since 2.7.1 the kept edges are a cost rather than a lock, so this mode reaches the count you asked for. See Hard Surface Preservation. The Preserve group opens.

Custom

Your own settings; every Advanced group opens. Changing any value a mode controls (Keep Normals, Keep UVs, Low Distortion, the four Preserve options, Sharp Angle, Clean Mesh and the Scan Repair switches) turns the menu into Custom (from Scan), and a Reset to Scan defaults button under it brings the mode's values back.

Protect Area, Bake, LODs, the amount and Keep Original are never touched by a mode.

Import and Crush

The file browser has the same Mode menu. In Auto, the engine looks at the file before it crushes it, which adds about the time it takes to read the file (a second or so for a 500 MB OBJ); the report says which mode it picked and what it found. See Import and Crush.
